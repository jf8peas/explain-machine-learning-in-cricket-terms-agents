4 · WHAT YOU SEE

Your browser keeps every step, so you can pause and rewind without running the agent again.

ScoreboardWhat everyone sees: one question, one picture, one sentence.
CommentaryOpened with "Show me everything": how and why.
ScorebookEvery model tried, the data and the full graph state.
COMMENTARY
Each step arrives as a small event while the agent is still working. The page replays them at a readable pace. Two scores less than 0.5 runs apart are called too close to call.
The graph, the replay and the data grid are general-purpose components, written so the next seven apps can reuse them.
