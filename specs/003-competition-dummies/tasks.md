## Implementation strategy

1. **Safe first step**: T001 and T002 give a baseline and a golden file before anything changes.
2. **Data first**: Phases 2 and 3 produce a verified table, manifest and script. Stop and review the data diff (T011) before touching the web side.
3. **Visible next**: US1, then US2 for the explanation, then US3 for the CSV check.
4. **Finish**: boundary tests, docs, a full run, and the human review of the explanation.
5. Throughout: `features.py`, `regression.py`, the tune loop, the explanation, `graph-replay`, the results card, the try-your-own form and the Working tab are not touched.
