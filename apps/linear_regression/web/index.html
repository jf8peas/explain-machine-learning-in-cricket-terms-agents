Can an agent beat the TV projected score?

After 10 overs of a T20 first innings, the broadcaster projects a final total by multiplying the current run rate by 20 overs. Watch an agent try something smarter, one step at a time: it learns from past innings how runs and wickets at the halfway mark turn into a final total, then tests itself on a year it has never seen.
