3 · Understand the data

Summary statistics from before the test brief the AI agent. Nothing is fitted here.

5–6 · Choose and fit

An idea is four choices: clues, seasons, recency weighting and innings. The agent proposes up to six; the brute-force search tries every combination of settings. Code scores each on the three check seasons.

7 · Final assessment

The chosen model is scored once on the test season. The goal is met when it beats the broadcaster's projection by at least not available right now runs.
